From Ammamma’s kitchen to yours

A small kitchen.
A lot of love.

We started Ammamma’s in 2019 with a simple mission: to bring the authentic taste of traditional Indian kitchens to modern homes, without compromises.

What began as a small kitchen experiment has grown into a brand bringing the taste of grandmother’s cooking to homes across India. Pickles, spice powders, chapatis, parotas and more: familiar food for the way we live today.
